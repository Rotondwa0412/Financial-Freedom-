# Financial-Freedom-
Why is it a challenge to secure financial freedom?
First of all, it is important for everyone to understand what financial freedom is. It can be defined as Having enough savings, investments and passive income to cover one's expenses. When you acquire financial freedom,  working a 9 to 5 becomes a choice. 
Let's breakdown some of the things that may contribution to financial strain. Most of our school did/do not teach personal finance. This leaves individuals ill-equipped to understand budgeting, tax structures and debt management.
The mindset  gaps- some people believe wealth is not meant for them but for other people. This mentality prevents individuals from taking steps to build assets.
Other people are "Present bias", they simply have undefined goals and they are not patient enough to build long term financial goals or wealth. To them, spending is life and "what I feel I want in the Present,  I get"
when income increases, people frequently elevate their standard of living. eg, upgrading houses,cars and buying luxurious goods instead of expanding the gap between what they earn and what they spend. 
The rising cost of living such as rapid increase in expenses of healthcare, education and houses continuously erode disposable incomes.
There is a lot to unpack about the challenges that lead to one not having financial freedom. The most important part of it all is to protect your mental health and your whole being, financial freedom can serve as protection.  In conclusion, Spend within your means, set financial goals,  invest in assets than liabilities, protect your wealth, keep track of your cash flow and try to eliminate high-interest rate debt. Is it really a challenge to Secure that financial freedom?
